The Story

The debut from Atlanta’s Bobby Ray Simmons Jr. arrived on a wave of genre-blending hype, with critics comparing his fearless mix of rap, pop, rock and soul to André 3000 and Kanye West. It earned generally favorable reviews and a Grammy nomination for Best Rap Album.

The rollout broke the rules. A mixtape bonus track, “Nothin’ on You,” took off so fast that Atlantic moved the album up nearly a month. It debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and went on to 2× Platinum, launching the careers of both B.o.B and a then-unknown Bruno Mars.

Its guest list told the whole story — Hayley Williams of Paramore, Rivers Cuomo of Weezer, Eminem, Janelle Monáe, Lupe Fiasco and label boss T.I. — a pop-crossover, rock-adjacent record that could only have come from Atlanta’s Grand Hustle camp.

The Patchwerk Connection

Patchwerk Recording Studios is listed as the primary studio for The Adventures of Bobby Ray — fitting for an Atlanta artist on T.I.’s hometown Grand Hustle label. The album is a documented part of Patchwerk’s catalog.

Per-track engineering credits for this album are lightly documented in public sources and are pending internal verification against Patchwerk’s session archive.

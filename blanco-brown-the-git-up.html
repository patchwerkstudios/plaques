Blanco Brown

The Question

Blanco Brown released “The Git Up” as his debut single on 3 May 2019, through TrailerTrapMusic LLC / BBR Music Group (BMG / Broken Bow); the song spent twelve weeks on the Hot Country Songs chart, hit No. 1, and went multi-platinum.

Of 33,841 transcribed Patchwerk session reports searched for Blanco Brown, exactly one comes back as a possible match — and it doesn’t settle anything.

On 21 February 2019, Studio 9000 hosted a two-hour listening session, 7pm to 9pm. Both the client and the artist fields are filled in the same way: “Blanco.” No last name. The engineer, written clearly, is Daniel “Tugg” Tuggerson. The project line reads simply “Listening.” No song title is written anywhere on the form — and no invoice number, either. No rentals, no tape or drive counts, no notes. A client signature is present but illegible.

The date sits about ten weeks before Blanco Brown released “The Git Up” as his debut single on 3 May 2019. That timing is suggestive. It is also all this sheet offers: a first name that matches, a date that fits a plausible window, and nothing else — no album track, no last name, no label billing that ties back to BMG, Broken Bow or TrailerTrapMusic LLC. The archive’s own research notes call this “the one weak match” in the whole 18-album search, and treat it as unconfirmed. This page does the same.

What the Sheet Shows

The Honest Answer

Did Blanco Brown record “The Git Up” at Patchwerk Recording Studios?

Patchwerk’s paperwork cannot confirm that. What exists is a single session report from 21 February 2019 — a two-hour listening session in Studio 9000 with both the client and artist written only as “Blanco,” no last name, no song title, engineer Daniel “Tugg” Tuggerson. The date falls about ten weeks before Blanco Brown released “The Git Up,” which is suggestive but not proof; nothing on the sheet identifies the artist beyond that one first name.

The Sheet Itself

What does the Patchwerk session report from 21 February 2019 actually say?

The 21 February 2019 Patchwerk session report logs a two-hour listening session in Studio 9000 from 7pm to 9pm. Client: “Blanco.” Artist: “Blanco.” Engineer: Daniel “Tugg” Tuggerson. Project: “Listening.” No producer, no song title, no invoice number, no rentals and no materials are recorded anywhere on the form — it is the sparsest kind of entry the studio kept.

Why It’s Unconfirmed

Why isn’t this session officially credited to Blanco Brown?

This 21 February 2019 Patchwerk session isn’t credited to Blanco Brown by name because the sheet never writes a full name, a song title, or a label. It is what the studio’s own research classifies as a Tier B entry — a listening session placed inside a plausible date window by a first name and a room, with none of the title-on-the-page evidence that confirms most other sessions in the archive.

The Timing

How close is the 21 February 2019 Patchwerk session to the release of “The Git Up”?

The 21 February 2019 session sits roughly ten weeks before Blanco Brown released “The Git Up” as his debut single on 3 May 2019, and about fourteen weeks before his self-titled debut EP followed on 31 May. If the sheet is his, it would place him at Patchwerk during the run-up to the song that became a multi-platinum, chart-topping country-rap crossover. If it isn’t, the timing is coincidence.

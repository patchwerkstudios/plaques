The Story

Bobby Ray Simmons Jr. was still an unsigned Decatur rapper working the mixtape circuit before B.o.B Presents: The Adventures of Bobby Ray shipped on Grand Hustle and Atlantic on 27 April 2010. The album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and went 2× Platinum, carried by two singles that both went to No. 1: “Nothin’ On You” and “Airplanes.”

Patchwerk’s own session reports place both of those songs in the building. Mike Wilson engineered “Nothin’ On You” in Studio 995 on 25 October 2009, an overnight session with Bruno Mars — credited on the sheet simply as “Bruno” — producing. Five months later and five weeks before the album’s release, Wilson tracked “Airplanes” in Studio 1019 with DJ Frank E, on a rented U-47 microphone, and the session report even logs the file handoff as its own line item: “U Send It Upload.”

Twelve sessions in all, most of them in 1019, running from an anonymous eight-hour vocal block on 9 January 2009 — thirteen months before he had a hit record — through the Airplanes upload in March 2010: a room B.o.B kept coming back to on the way up.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was B.o.B’s The Adventures of Bobby Ray recorded?

B.o.B recorded sessions for The Adventures of Bobby Ray at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta, Georgia. Patchwerk’s own daily session reports document 12 sessions between January 2009 and March 2010, split between Studio 995 and Studio 1019, engineered mostly by Mike Wilson — including the tracking dates for both of the album’s No. 1 singles.

The First Single

Where was B.o.B’s “Nothin’ On You” recorded?

“Nothin’ On You” was recorded at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Studio 995 on 25 October 2009, six months before The Adventures of Bobby Ray was released. Mike Wilson engineered an 11½-hour overnight session, 2pm to 1:30am, with the sheet listing the producer simply as “Bruno” — Bruno Mars, whose hook made the song B.o.B’s first No. 1.

The Second Single

Where and when was B.o.B’s “Airplanes” recorded?

“Airplanes” was tracked at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Studio 1019 on 18 March 2010, five weeks before the album shipped. Mike Wilson engineered with DJ Frank E producing, 6:30pm to 2am, on a U-47 microphone rented in from Eclipse. The session report logs the vocal pass and then, as its own line item, the file transfer: “U Send It Upload,” within that same 7½-hour session — the sheet gives one combined hours total, not a separate tally for the upload.

Before the Fame

Was B.o.B recording at Patchwerk before he was famous?

Yes — the earliest B.o.B sheet in Patchwerk’s binders is dated 9 January 2009, more than a year before “Airplanes” made him a household name. The report is almost bare: Studio 1019, eight hours and eleven minutes, project logged only as “Vocals,” no producer or song title written down — a routine working day for an artist nobody outside Atlanta had heard of yet.

Other Sessions

What other songs did B.o.B record at Patchwerk besides the singles?

Patchwerk’s reports also log a session for “Dream On Me” on 13 October 2009 in Studio 995 with Mike Wilson — a sheet the studio’s own staff photographed three separate times — and one for “We Don’t Sleep” on 8 November 2009. Neither title appears on the final tracklist of The Adventures of Bobby Ray; either way, the sessions themselves are on the record.

Why it matters

Both of B.o.B’s No. 1 singles — a full year apart, in two different rooms — were tracked at Patchwerk before either one had charted. The paperwork was written by someone who had no idea yet which songs would matter.

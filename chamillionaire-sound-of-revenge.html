The Story

Chamillionaire’s solo debut arrived 22 November 2005 on his own Chamillitary Entertainment through Universal, and went platinum on the strength of the Grammy-winning single “Ridin’.”

Patchwerk’s own sheets don’t cover that single — what they document is a different track’s vocal session, six months before release, and two short post-release visits the following spring. On 1 May 2005, engineer “Exit” — the only name this sheet gives him — booked a ten-hour overnight in Studio 9000 for Universal, producer Mannie Fresh, artist logged simply “Chamillionaire.” The song on the sheet: “Fly,” released as Fly as the Sky, track thirteen on the finished album, featuring Lil Wayne.

Two more sheets follow the record into 2006, after release: a nine-hour vocal session on 27 April and a thirty-minute dub-room copy job on 29 April, both billed to Universal for a song logged as “Grown and Sexy” — the album’s own track nine, being revisited for additional vocals and archive copies months after The Sound of Revenge had already shipped.

Confidence here is Medium: all three sheets carry Chamillionaire’s name, Universal’s billing and song titles that match the released album directly, but the total paper trail is three sheets across just under twelve months (1 May 2005 to 29 April 2006), not a documented studio residency.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Chamillionaire’s The Sound of Revenge recorded?

Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta hosted at least part of the work on Chamillionaire’s 2005 album The Sound of Revenge, released on his own Chamillitary Entertainment through Universal Records. Patchwerk’s session reports document three sessions tied to the album, from a pre-release vocal date in May 2005 to two post-release sessions in April 2006.

The Song

What song did Chamillionaire record at Patchwerk before The Sound of Revenge came out?

Chamillionaire recorded “Fly” — released on The Sound of Revenge as Fly as the Sky, featuring Lil Wayne — at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta on 1 May 2005, six and a half months before the album came out. The session report logs a ten-hour overnight in Studio 9000, engineer “Exit” (the sheet gives no other name), producer Mannie Fresh, billed to Universal.

After Release

Did Chamillionaire keep working on the album after it came out?

Yes. Two Patchwerk sessions in April 2006, five months after The Sound of Revenge shipped, revisited its ninth track, “Grown and Sexy” — a nine-hour vocal session on 27 April with engineer Mike W., followed by a thirty-minute dub-room job on 29 April making backup copies of the Pro Tools session and audio.

How Sure Are We

How confident is the match between these Patchwerk sessions and The Sound of Revenge?

Medium confidence. All three Patchwerk sheets name Chamillionaire directly, bill Universal Records, and log song titles — “Fly” and “Grown and Sexy” — that match tracks on the released album, but the total record is three sheets spread across just under twelve months, not a continuous documented session block.

The Producer

Who produced Chamillionaire’s Patchwerk session for “Fly”?

Mannie Fresh, the New Orleans producer and former in-house beatmaker for Cash Money Records, is logged as producer on Patchwerk’s 1 May 2005 session for “Fly,” with engineer “Exit” (as recorded on this sheet) and Jamie Newman assisting in Studio 9000.

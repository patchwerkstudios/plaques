The Story

Evolver was John Legend’s third studio album, released on G.O.O.D. Music and Columbia on 28 October 2008. It debuted at No. 4 on the Billboard 200 on sales of 132,823 copies, was later certified Platinum by the RIAA, and carried guest turns from Brandy, Estelle and André 3000.

Every one of Patchwerk’s seven session reports for the record is Tier A — a confirmed song title on the sheet, no guesswork — and every one runs through Studio 9000 with Mike Wilson engineering. The work centers on “Green Light,” which Patchwerk tracked or mixed four separate times over fourteen months: a first pass in January 2007, more than a year before the song was announced as a single; a second on New Year’s Eve 2007, with André 3000 himself in the building and producer credit split “Andre 3000 / KP” on the sheet; a third pass folded into a marathon February 2008 session alongside five other tracks; and a fourth — the mix — spread across two March 2008 dates under spec-deal terms.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was John Legend’s Evolver recorded?

John Legend recorded sessions for Evolver at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta, Georgia. Patchwerk’s own daily session reports document seven sessions, every one of them in Studio 9000 with engineer Mike Wilson, running from January 2007 through the final mix in March 2008.

New Year’s Eve

Did André 3000 really record with John Legend on New Year’s Eve?

Yes — on 31 December 2007, Patchwerk logged a three-hour session in Studio 9000 for “Green Light Malay,” with the artist line reading “John Legend / ft Andre 3000” and the producer line reading “Andre 3000 / KP.” Mike Wilson engineered, Denny Ogle assisted, and the session ran 8pm to 11pm — New Year’s Eve itself, on what would become Evolver’s first single.

Other Songs

What other Evolver songs were recorded at Patchwerk?

Beyond “Green Light,” Patchwerk’s February 2008 sessions name Everybody Knows, Can’t Be My Lover, It’s Over, No Other Love, Floating Away and Good Morning — the last with live horns tracked on 18 February. A single 2 February session logged six song titles worked in one sitting, plus overflow beats noted only as “Rich Beat w/ ref vox” and “Rich Beat Ins.”

Why it matters

“Green Light” came back to the same Patchwerk room four times over fourteen months — a first pass in January 2007, once with André 3000 on New Year’s Eve, once buried in a six-song marathon, and once more to finish the mix in March 2008. The paperwork was the only thing keeping track.

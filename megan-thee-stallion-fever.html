The Story

In July 2018, Megan Thee Stallion was a year removed from her first viral freestyles and still ten months from Fever, the mixtape Billboard would later name one of the best albums of 2019.

She spent three days that month in Patchwerk’s Studio 9000, tracking songs under her label 1501 Certified and its A&R T Farris with engineer Luke Campolieta — who is logged as producer, not engineer, on the last of the three sessions. All three session reports share a single invoice number — 30873 — run across three separate visits: 9, 10 and 12 July 2018. On the 9th, the sheet lists three song titles — “Secret,” “Top Rock” and a title written phonetically as “Regler”, almost certainly Realer, the song that opens the finished mixtape. The 10th produced “Sex Talk” and “Make Em Mad”; the 12th produced “Money Good” and “Best You’ve Ever Had.” Four of those titles — Realer, Sex Talk, Money Good and Best You Ever Had — are on the released tracklist.

Every sheet ends the same way: a hard drive taken by the client, a backup logged, a signature that’s hard to read. Nothing here says this would matter yet.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Megan Thee Stallion’s Fever recorded?

Megan Thee Stallion recorded songs for Fever at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta, Georgia. Patchwerk’s own session reports document three visits to Studio 9000 in July 2018 — 9, 10 and 12 July — billed to her label 1501 Certified under a single invoice, with Luke Campolieta engineering the first two and producing the third.

The Timing

When did Megan Thee Stallion record Fever songs at Patchwerk?

Megan Thee Stallion tracked at Patchwerk Recording Studios from 9–12 July 2018, roughly ten months before Fever was released on 17 May 2019. At the time she had one chart entry to her name; the mixtape that would make her a star was still being written on these sheets.

The Songs

Which Fever songs were tracked at Patchwerk Recording Studios?

Four songs on the released version of Fever can be traced to Patchwerk Recording Studios by name: “Realer,” “Sex Talk,” “Money Good” and “Best You Ever Had.” The 9 July sheet writes Realer phonetically as “Regler” and lists two more working titles, “Secret” and “Top Rock,” that don’t appear on the finished tracklist.

The Engineer

Who engineered Megan Thee Stallion’s sessions at Patchwerk?

Luke Campolieta engineered two of Megan Thee Stallion’s three logged sessions for Fever at Patchwerk Recording Studios — the 9 and 10 July dates, six and seven hours respectively. On the third, 12 July, the sheet moves him to the producer field and leaves the engineer line blank, all three days out of Studio 9000.

The Paper Trail

What does invoice #30873 show about Megan Thee Stallion’s Fever sessions?

Invoice #30873 is the single billing record Patchwerk Recording Studios used to cover Megan Thee Stallion’s three Fever sessions across 9, 10 and 12 July 2018 — a multi-day block booking rather than three separate charges, run through her label 1501 Certified with a single hard-drive backup taken by the client each night.

The Story

Missy Elliott’s fourth studio album, Under Construction, arrived on The Goldmind / Elektra on 12 November 2002, reached No. 3 on the Billboard 200, and has been certified 2× Platinum by the RIAA.

Patchwerk’s two session reports for Under Construction sit five days apart in September 2002, and together they cover the exact week that Beyoncé also worked in this building — a coincidence the paperwork itself connects.

The first, on 3 September, is billed to Elektra Records with DaMizza and Timbaland producing, engineer Josh Butler and assistant Cory Williams tracking vocals for two hours. The sheet’s song field just says “title not on sheet,” but the notes name it plainly: “Double Dutch.” It doesn’t appear on the finished album under that name — a working title for something that either changed or didn’t make the cut.

The second, on 8 September, is the real find. The artist line reads “Missy Elliot feat. Beyonce Knowles,” producer Missy Elliott, engineer Carlos Bedoya, assistant Steve Fisher, ten hours of tracking vocals from 11am to 9pm. The song title on the sheet is “Nothin Out There” — released as “Nothing Out There for Me,” track eight on Under Construction. The materials line at the bottom releases one Pro Tools CD to Carlos and two audio CDs — to “Missy + Beyonce” by name. Three days later, on 11 September, Beyoncé filed her own Elektra-billed session at Patchwerk logging the same song under the title “Ain’t Nothing Out There.”

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Missy Elliott’s Under Construction recorded?

Missy Elliott worked on Under Construction at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta, Georgia, among other studios credited on the finished album. Patchwerk’s own session reports document two sessions in Studio 995 in September 2002; the 3 September sheet is billed to Elektra Records, and the 8 September sheet’s client field is blank and partly illegible on the original.

Beyoncé in the Building

Was Beyoncé at Patchwerk when Missy Elliott recorded “Nothing Out There for Me”?

Yes — Patchwerk’s session report for 8 September 2002 lists the artist as “Missy Elliot feat. Beyonce Knowles” and releases materials from the ten-hour session to “Missy + Beyonce” by name, placing Beyoncé in the building during the vocal tracking for Under Construction’s “Nothing Out There for Me.” Three days later, Beyoncé filed her own Elektra-billed Patchwerk session logging the same song.

The Song

When was Missy Elliott’s “Nothing Out There for Me” recorded?

“Nothing Out There for Me,” track eight on Under Construction, was tracked at Patchwerk Recording Studios on 8 September 2002 — about ten weeks before the album’s 12 November release. Engineer Carlos Bedoya ran the ten-hour session, 11am to 9pm, with Steve Fisher assisting, and the sheet writes the title as “Nothin Out There.”

The Engineers

Who engineered Missy Elliott’s Patchwerk sessions for Under Construction?

Carlos Bedoya and Josh Butler engineered Missy Elliott’s Under Construction sessions at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta in September 2002. Bedoya engineered the 8 September session for “Nothing Out There for Me,” assisted by Steve Fisher; Butler engineered a 3 September session, assisted by Cory Williams, for a track logged as “Double Dutch” and produced by DaMizza and Timbaland.

One Week, Two Records

Did Missy Elliott and Beyoncé record at Patchwerk during the same week?

Yes. Missy Elliott’s session for Under Construction on 8 September 2002 and Beyoncé’s two sessions for Dangerously in Love on 11 September 2002 fall in the same week at Patchwerk Recording Studios, in the same room, and one Missy sheet names Beyoncé directly as a featured artist in the building.

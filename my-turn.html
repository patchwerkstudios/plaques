The Story

My Turn was Lil Baby’s coronation. The Atlanta rapper’s second album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, returned to the top months later, and logged five non-consecutive weeks at No. 1 — finishing as the single most-consumed album in the United States for all of 2020.

Its fifth single, “The Bigger Picture,” arrived in June 2020 as a George Floyd protest anthem, debuting at No. 3 on the Hot 100 — Lil Baby’s highest-ever chart entry — and earning two Grammy nominations. The album placed twelve songs on the Hot 100 in its first week alone.

Recorded across Atlanta largely through 2019, My Turn fused emotive, melodic delivery with heavy trap production. As Lil Baby put it: “Everybody else had a lil’ turn. It’s my turn now.”

The Patchwerk Connection

My Turn was recorded in part at Patchwerk Recording Studios, with tracking engineer Matthew “Mattazik Muzik” Robinson among the Atlanta rooms that shaped the year’s biggest album. (Mixing was completed elsewhere.)

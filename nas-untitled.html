The Story

Nas released his ninth studio album on 15 July 2008 with no title printed anywhere — a plain black cover, sold simply as Nas or Untitled. It entered the Billboard 200 at No. 1, his eighth consecutive album to do so, and was certified Gold.

Patchwerk logged two sessions on it, both in Studio 995 and both overnight. On 11 May 2007, engineer Kori Anders tracked two beats with a producer written on the sheet only as “Lex” — 7pm to 5am, ten hours, with a note that the room’s SE-1 screen wasn’t working that night.

Thirteen months later, on 5 June 2008, Mike Wilson brought a live string section into the same room for eleven hours, 6pm to 5am, with DJ Toomp producing and Denny Ogle assisting. An MPC 160, an ASR-10 and a Fantom X7 were rented in for the date — a string session that ran until five in the morning, in a room usually booked for rap vocals.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Nas’s Untitled album recorded?

Nas worked on his 2008 self-titled album, released as Untitled by Def Jam Recordings, at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta. Patchwerk’s own session reports log two sessions for the record between 11 May 2007 and 5 June 2008, all in Studio 995.

The Session

What happened at Patchwerk on 5 June 2008?

On 5 June 2008, Nas brought a live string section into Patchwerk’s Studio 995. Engineer Mike Wilson ran the eleven-hour overnight session, 6pm to 5am, with Denny Ogle assisting and DJ Toomp producing; an MPC 160, an ASR-10 and a Fantom X7 were rented in for the date.

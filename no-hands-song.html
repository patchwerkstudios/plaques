The Story

“No Hands” was built in about five minutes: producer Drumma Boy cut the beat during a spring 2010 session tied to Gucci Mane’s release from jail, Roscoe Dash improvised the now-iconic “Girl, you got me moving” hook on the spot, and Wale wrote and delivered his verse the same day. Released 17 August 2010 as the second single from Waka Flocka Flame’s debut album Flockaveli, it peaked at No. 13 on the Billboard Hot 100 and No. 1 on Hot Rap Songs, won Best Club Banger at the 2011 BET Hip-Hop Awards, and was certified Diamond by the RIAA in July 2023 — Waka Flocka’s first Diamond record and, to date, his signature song.

Patchwerk’s own paperwork for “No Hands” is thin, and that is the honest story. The studio’s only session report naming the song is a half-hour backup job, not a tracking or mixing date: on 23 August 2010, six days after the single’s release, engineer Brian Friesen logged thirty minutes in Studio 1019 to make a “Backup DVD Copy” of the finished track. Where and when “No Hands” was actually written, tracked and mixed isn’t in Patchwerk’s records at all.

What Patchwerk’s sheets document at length instead is the album’s other single: five sessions between January and March 2010 for “O Let’s Do It,” Flockaveli’s actual lead single, plus a likely sixth, untitled session from November 2009. That studio time — the real story behind Patchwerk’s Waka Flocka Flame plaque — is on the studio’s Flockaveli album page, alongside a photo of the Triple Platinum award on the wall for this song.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Waka Flocka Flame’s “No Hands” recorded?

Patchwerk Recording Studios’ paperwork doesn’t document where “No Hands” was recorded — the studio’s only session report naming the song is a backup copy, not a tracking date. On 23 August 2010, engineer Brian Friesen spent thirty minutes in Studio 1019 making a “Backup DVD Copy” of the finished song, six days after its 17 August release. What survives in the archive is proof the studio held a copy of the record, not proof it was made there.

The Engineer

Which Patchwerk engineer logged Waka Flocka Flame’s “No Hands”?

The only Patchwerk engineer on record for “No Hands” is Brian Friesen, who logged the 23 August 2010 session in Studio 1019 as “Copy backup Song No Hands / Backup DVD Copy — No Hands.” No engineer, producer or session is on file at Patchwerk for actually recording or mixing the song.

The Actual Studio Time

Did Waka Flocka Flame record at Patchwerk Recording Studios for Flockaveli?

Yes — just not on “No Hands.” Patchwerk’s session reports document five sessions between January and March 2010 for “O Let’s Do It,” the album’s other single, plus a likely sixth from November 2009, all engineered by Kori Anders in Studio 995. See Patchwerk’s Flockaveli album page for that story, including a handwritten engineer’s note about waiting on P. Diddy to approve a remix.

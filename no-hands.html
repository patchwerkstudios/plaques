The Story

“No Hands” was born on the fly. In spring 2010, during a session tied to Gucci Mane’s release from jail, producer Drumma Boy built the beat in about five minutes. Roscoe Dash was pushed into the booth to improvise the now-iconic “Girl, you got me moving” hook, and Wale wrote and delivered the featured verse on the spot.

Released that August, it became Waka Flocka’s signature record — peaking at No. 13 on the Billboard Hot 100, No. 1 on Hot Rap Songs, and winning Best Club Banger at the 2011 BET Hip-Hop Awards. It spawned a wave of remixes, from T-Pain’s T-Mix to countless club edits.

In July 2023 the RIAA certified it Diamond — ten million units — making it Waka Flocka’s first Diamond record and cementing it as a foundational Atlanta trap crossover.

The Patchwerk Connection

By Drumma Boy’s own account, the beat and hook for “No Hands” were created live in a Patchwerk room — conjured in minutes during a packed session. The released master was later finished by engineer Finis “KY” White at S-Line, but this Diamond-selling anthem first came to life at Patchwerk.

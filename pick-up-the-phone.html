The Story

“Pick Up the Phone” was released on 21 July 2016 and ended up on two albums at once — Travis Scott’s Birds in the Trap Sing McKnight and Young Thug’s Jeffery — with Quavo featured and Starrah on the hook. It has since been certified five-times Platinum.

Patchwerk’s books hold a session report dated 22 March 2016 — four months before the single was released. Studio 9000, client and artist both written “Young Thug,” engineer Mathew “Stafa” Carter, 8pm to 3:30am, seven and a half hours. The project column reads “Tracking / Edits.” The song column reads, simply, “Pick Up.” Backup left at Patchwerk on a flash drive; work saved to Work Drive 1.

The sheet stops short of the full title, so it is offered here as what it is — a tracking and editing session on a song written down as “Pick Up,” sixteen weeks before “Pick Up the Phone” came out.

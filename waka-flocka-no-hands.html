The Story

Waka Flocka Flame’s debut album Flockaveli is remembered chiefly for one song: “No Hands,” the Drumma Boy-produced strip-club anthem featuring Roscoe Dash and Wale that has since sold more than ten million units and earned Waka his first RIAA Diamond certification. It’s the plaque hanging on Patchwerk’s wall. But the studio’s own paperwork for the album tells a narrower, more specific story than that platinum frame suggests.

The song Patchwerk’s sheets actually document, session after session, is the album’s real lead single: “O Let’s Do It,” first released in 2009. Between November 2009 and March 2010, engineer Kori Anders — spelled “Anders” on some sheets, “Andes” on others — logged five sessions in Studio 995 that name the song directly: clean edits, a remix, a video-edit pass, and a 29 January 2010 recall session whose project-description line explains an extra hour in the engineer’s own hand: “Had to wait for P. Diddy to approve all changes.” A sixth, earlier sheet — 12 November 2009, the earliest in the file — logs the same engineer and room for “Ruff mixes,” but its title field is blank, so it’s counted here as connected, not confirmed. Diddy, along with Rick Ross and Gucci Mane, guests on the official “O Let’s Do It” remix — the 29 January sheet is Patchwerk’s paper trail for getting that verse locked in.

By contrast, Patchwerk’s only sheet for “No Hands” itself is a half-hour housekeeping job, not a tracking or mixing date: 23 August 2010, Studio 1019, engineer Brian Friesen, “Copy backup Song No Hands / Backup DVD Copy — No Hands” — made six days after the single’s 17 August release. It’s proof of custody, not proof of creation. A postscript closes out the run: on 16 November 2010, Kori Anders logged a Tier B tracking session for “Gucci Mane (Waka, Dunk & G),” working four songs not credited to Flockaveli — evidence the relationship between Waka’s camp and Patchwerk kept going past the album’s release.

The Recording

No Hands

Was Waka Flocka Flame’s “No Hands” recorded at Patchwerk Recording Studios?

Patchwerk Recording Studios’ only documented session for “No Hands” is a backup copy, not a tracking date: on 23 August 2010, engineer Brian Friesen spent thirty minutes in Studio 1019 making a “Backup DVD Copy” of the song, six days after its 17 August release. The song itself — produced by Drumma Boy, featuring Roscoe Dash and Wale — is now RIAA Diamond-certified with more than 10 million units sold, Waka’s best-selling single. Patchwerk’s paperwork documents its archiving, not its creation.

The Lead Single

Where was Waka Flocka Flame’s “O Let’s Do It” recorded?

“O Let’s Do It,” the actual lead single from Flockaveli, was worked on at Patchwerk Recording Studios across five sessions that name it directly between January and March 2010, all in Studio 995 with engineer Kori Anders (the sheets spell it both “Anders” and “Andes”) — plus a likely sixth, on 12 November 2009, logged only as “Ruff mixes” with no title on the sheet. The named sessions ran from clean edits through a full remix to a video-edit pass — more studio time than any other Flockaveli song in Patchwerk’s records.

The P. Diddy Note

Why does a Waka Flocka Flame session report mention P. Diddy?

A 29 January 2010 Patchwerk session report for the “O Let’s Do It” remix explains a scheduling delay in the engineer’s own handwriting: “# Had to wait for P. Diddy to approve all changes is the reason for the extra hour.” Diddy features on the official remix to “O Let’s Do It” alongside Rick Ross and Gucci Mane, and this recall session — logged at four hours instead of the usual three — is Patchwerk’s paper trail for getting his verse signed off.

The Plaque

What does the Waka Flocka Flame plaque at Patchwerk commemorate?

The framed award on Patchwerk’s wall is a Triple Platinum plaque presented to Patchwerk Recording Studios by Warner Bros. Records, commemorating RIAA-certified sales of more than 3,000,000 digital downloads of the 1017 Brick Squad / Warner Bros. single “No Hands” featuring Roscoe Dash and Wale. The song has since been upgraded to Diamond status by the RIAA for surpassing ten million units.

Why it matters

“No Hands” became Waka Flocka Flame’s signature record and, in 2023, his first Diamond single — but Patchwerk’s own paperwork for it is a half-hour backup copy, not a studio date. The real studio time went to the song that actually opened the album’s story, “O Let’s Do It,” five sessions deep by name and a sixth, untitled one before that, with a handwritten note explaining why they were waiting on P. Diddy.

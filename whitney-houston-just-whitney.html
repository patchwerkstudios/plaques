The Story

Just Whitney was released on Arista Records on 10 December 2002, Whitney Houston’s fifth studio album and her first since re-signing with the label. It debuted at No. 9 on the Billboard 200 with the biggest first-week sales of her Nielsen SoundScan-era career, and was certified Platinum by the RIAA.

Patchwerk’s own session reports document six sessions for the record between May and August 2002, split between Studio 995 and Studio 9000. Josh Butler engineered the lead single, “Whatchulookinat,” across an overnight date on 26 May, and Steve Fisher and Stephen George worked the sessions that followed through the summer.

The last of the six sheets, dated 17 August 2002, logs a 28-hour mix — noon on a Saturday to 4pm the following Sunday — for “My Love,” credited on the sheet as “Whitney Houston & Bobby Brown,” with a rental list of outboard gear that runs to a dozen lines.

The Recording

The Studio

Where was Whitney Houston’s Just Whitney recorded?

Whitney Houston recorded sessions for Just Whitney at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Atlanta, Georgia. Patchwerk’s own daily session reports document six sessions between May and August 2002, in Studio 995 and Studio 9000, billed to Arista Records and engineered by Josh Butler, Steve Fisher, Stephen George and Mike Wilson.

The Lead Single

Where was Whitney Houston’s “Whatchulookinat” recorded?

“Whatchulookinat,” the lead single from Just Whitney, was tracked at Patchwerk Recording Studios in Studio 995 on 26 May 2002. Engineer Josh Butler ran a 17½-hour overnight session, noon to 5:30am, with assistant Steve Fisher — vocals for “Whatcha Lookin’ At” and a second, then-untitled song logged on the sheet only as “Easy.”

Bobby Brown

Was Bobby Brown involved in the Patchwerk sessions for Just Whitney?

Yes — Bobby Brown is named on four of Patchwerk’s six session reports for Just Whitney, including as producer on a 19 May 2002 date in Studio 9000 and as co-credited artist on the 17 August mix of “My Love,” engineered by Stephen George. The 19 May sheet carries a handwritten red marginal note restricting the song’s copies from release — “not finished yet,” per a note attributed to R. Jerkins.

The Long Mix

How long did Patchwerk spend mixing “My Love” for Just Whitney?

The mix of “My Love” ran 28 hours at Patchwerk Recording Studios, from noon on Saturday, 17 August 2002 to 4pm the following Sunday. Engineer Stephen George worked with assistant Steve Fisher and producers Teddy Bishop and Gordon Chambers, and the session report’s rental list — a Manley Variable-Mu compressor, an AMS DMX, an Eventide Orville, an Apogee AD-1000 among a dozen other lines — is the longest of any sheet in the album’s file.

Cee-Lo

Did Cee-Lo appear on any Just Whitney sessions at Patchwerk?

Yes — a 13 August 2002 Patchwerk session report lists the artist as “Whitney Houston feat. Cee-Lo,” tracking vocals for a remix of “Whatcha Lookin’ At.” Steve Fisher engineered with Cory Williams assisting, in a three-hour Studio 995 session running 3pm to 6pm.

Why it matters

Every producer credited on Patchwerk’s own paperwork for this record — Bobby Brown on the 19 May session, Teddy Bishop and Gordon Chambers on the 28-hour mix of “My Love” — signed a session report to get there. The paperwork shows a working record: overnight sessions, engineers logging every disc and reel, a mix that ran a Saturday into a Sunday.
